## What Makes Corrugated Cardboard Different?

Unlike ordinary paperboard, corrugated cardboard contains a fluted layer positioned between flat sheets of linerboard. This arrangement creates a structured material with spaces between the layers.

The construction generally includes:

- **Outer linerboard:** Forms the exterior surface of the material.
- **Fluted medium:** Creates the wavy internal structure that contributes to cushioning and rigidity.
- **Inner linerboard:** Provides the opposite flat surface and completes the basic construction.

The fluted structure helps distribute pressure and provides useful resistance to everyday handling. Different flute profiles and board combinations are available depending on the packaging application.

## Common Corrugated Board Constructions

The number of fluted layers influences the thickness and structural characteristics of corrugated cardboard.

### Single Wall Corrugated Cardboard

Single-wall board contains one fluted layer between two linerboards. It is widely used for general shipping cartons, product packaging, and protective packaging applications.

### Double Wall Corrugated Cardboard

Double-wall construction combines two fluted layers with three linerboards. It provides additional thickness and support for packaging applications that require a more substantial structure.

### Triple Wall Corrugated Cardboard

Triple-wall board contains three fluted layers separated by four linerboards. It is considered for demanding packaging applications involving heavier products and specialized shipping requirements.

## How to Select the Appropriate Corrugated Material

The right corrugated construction depends on the product and the conditions it may encounter during storage and transportation.

**Start with Product Weight**

Lightweight merchandise and heavy industrial components have different packaging requirements. Consider the total packed weight before selecting a board construction.

**Evaluate Product Dimensions**

Measure the item carefully and determine whether a standard carton or a specialized box style is more suitable.

**Consider the Required Protection**

Fragile products may need additional cushioning, dividers, foam inserts, or other protective materials alongside the corrugated outer packaging.

**Review Transportation Conditions**

Stacking, handling, vibration, and transportation distance can influence the type of board and packaging arrangement required.

**Choose the Appropriate Flute Structure**

Different flute profiles affect board thickness, cushioning, and rigidity. The selection should reflect the intended use rather than relying on thickness alone.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Corrugated Cardboard

### 1. What is corrugated cardboard used for?

Corrugated cardboard is used to manufacture shipping boxes, protective sheets, dividers, pads, and other packaging components. It helps contain, separate, and protect products during storage and transportation.

### 2. What is the difference between cardboard and corrugated cardboard?

Cardboard is a broad term often used for paper-based packaging materials. Corrugated cardboard has a distinctive fluted layer between flat linerboards, creating a structured material commonly used for shipping and protective packaging.

### 3. Is corrugated cardboard suitable for heavy products?

Certain double-wall and triple-wall constructions can be used for heavier products. The appropriate selection depends on board grade, box design, product weight, and shipping conditions.

### 4. What are corrugated sheets used for?

Corrugated sheets can be used as pallet layer pads, product separators, protective covers, carton dividers, and internal packaging components.

### 5. Can corrugated cardboard be customized?

Yes. Corrugated material can be converted into different dimensions, box styles, die-cut shapes, and printed packaging designs according to application requirements.

### 6. Is corrugated cardboard recyclable?

Clean corrugated cardboard is generally recyclable through suitable paper and cardboard recycling programs. Local recycling rules and material contamination may affect acceptance.

### 7. Which corrugated construction should I choose?

Single-wall, double-wall, and triple-wall boards serve different packaging requirements. Consider the product's weight, dimensions, fragility, stacking needs, and transportation conditions when choosing a construction.
